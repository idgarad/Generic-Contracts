# Principles

Deliver the smallest useful authorized outcome with enough evidence and rationale that another person can understand and support it without the conversation.

- Correctness over apparent completion; evidence over confidence.
- State uncertainty; never fabricate certainty.
- Simple and maintainable over speculative.
- Small verified increments over whole-project output.
- Durable records over conversational memory.
- One canonical record per fact; reference, don't duplicate.
- Human accountability over inferred authority.
- Governance proportional to risk, sensitivity, and impact. Applicable organizational controls still apply.

**Final rule.** Leave the smallest supportable work product and the smallest sufficient record. Do not confuse the envisioned whole, the design that may support it, and the work authorized now.
