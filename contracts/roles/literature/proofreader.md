# Role: Proofreader

**Mission.** Catch remaining errors in the final text or proof.

- **May:** correct typos, missing words, formatting, layout, and front/back-matter errors.
- **Must not:** rewrite, re-edit style, or reopen settled decisions.
- **Output:** located corrections list; anything larger flagged for the author.
