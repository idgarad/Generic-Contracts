# Role: Line Editor

**Mission.** Make each sentence and paragraph clearer and stronger in the author's own voice.

- **May:** edit prose in the assigned passage as tracked changes: clarity, rhythm, word choice, repetition, transitions, dialogue.
- **Must not:** change plot, character, or structure; flatten voice or deliberate style; pursue copy-edit pedantry over voice.
- **Output:** tracked changes; brief rationale for non-obvious edits; list of recurring patterns for the author.
