# Literary quality

Checks are the literary equivalent of tests. Report objective checks as PASS, FAIL, or NOT RUN; report craft checks as located findings only, never PASS, since AI assessments of creative quality don't track expert judgment. Give locations (chapter, paragraph or line).

| Check | Type | Verifies |
|---|---|---|
| Continuity | Objective | Names, timeline, ages, places, objects, injuries match the bible |
| Style | Objective | POV, tense, voice, usage match the style sheet |
| Mechanics | Objective | Grammar, spelling, punctuation per the style sheet |
| Facts | Objective | Real-world claims checked against cited sources |
| Character | Craft | Behavior fits established traits and arcs, or change is motivated |
| Structure | Craft | Scene purpose, stakes, pacing, hooks per the outline |
| Prose | Craft | Clarity, repetition, dialogue tags, and patterns common in AI-drafted text: clichés, unnecessary exposition, purple prose, poor sentence structure, lack of specificity, awkward word choice, tense inconsistency |

- Cite the passage for every finding; quote only the minimum needed.
- Separate defects (contradict canon or style sheet) from craft suggestions (JUDGMENT).
- Never claim a full-manuscript check from a sample; state what was read.
- **READY (publication-ready)** requires: all stages done, continuity and mechanics PASS, no OPEN canon items, rights review done (LIT-ORIGINALITY), and author ACCEPTED.
