# Enterprise adoption and migration

**Adopt:** review contracts, configure agent discovery, bind the minimum workflow (WF-BINDING), name accountable owners, establish checks. Overlays MUST list actual applicable controls, not infer them from the word "enterprise".

**Migrate:**
1. Identify destination authorities and applicable controls.
2. Map record fields; preserve IDs or old-to-new references.
3. Migrate active work first; keep intent as intent, not tickets.
4. Verify requirements, criteria, decisions, authorization, and evidence survived.
5. Update bindings; confirm cutover.
6. Retire competing live records; keep required archives or pointers.

Migration MUST NOT expand scope, promote deferred ideas, lose evidence, or leave two live backlogs. No permanent dual-writing unless explicitly designed.
