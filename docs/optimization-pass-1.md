# Optimization Pass 1 (branch `ClaudeOptimizationPass1`)

**Goal:** across a large project, reduce token consumption, raise quality, and reduce hallucination.
**Rule applied:** change a contract only where a peer-reviewed finding supports the change; leave every other contract untouched.
**Date:** 2026-11-08.

## Sources (all peer-reviewed venues)

| # | Finding used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | LLM reasoning degrades as input grows, even around 3,000 tokens, far below context limits. | Levy, Jacoby, Goldberg. *Same Task, More Tokens.* ACL 2024. [ACL Anthology](https://preview.aclanthology.org/setup/2024.acl-long.818) |
| S2 | Repository context files (AGENTS.md style) raised inference cost by more than 20% with no task-success gain; LLM-generated files lowered success. Agents act on what the files say, which drives the extra cost. | Gloaguen, Mündler, Müller, Raychev, Vechev. *Evaluating AGENTS.md.* ICLR 2026 workshop. [ETH SRI](https://www.sri.inf.ethz.ch/publications/gloaguen2026agentsmd) |
| S3 | 55% (GPT-3.5) and 18% (GPT-4) of generated citations were fabricated. | Walters, Wilder. *Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT.* Scientific Reports 13, 2023. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-41032-5.pdf) |
| S4 | Code LLMs reference nonexistent packages: at least 5.2% (commercial) and 21.7% (open-source) of samples; 205,474 unique fake names. | Spracklen et al. *We Have a Package for You!* USENIX Security 2025. [USENIX](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/spracklen) |
| S5 | About 40% of 1,689 Copilot-generated programs were vulnerable (MITRE Top-25 CWE scenarios). | Pearce et al. *Asleep at the Keyboard?* IEEE S&P 2022. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.09293) |
| S6 | Intrinsic self-correction, with no external feedback, does not reliably improve reasoning and can make it worse. | Huang et al. *Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet.* ICLR 2024. [ICLR](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/18956) |
| S7 | LLM evaluators recognize and favor their own generations. | Panickssery, Bowman, Feng. NeurIPS 2024 (oral). [NeurIPS](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/7f1f0218e45f5414c79c0679633e47bc-Abstract-Conference.html) |
| S8 | LLM judges show position bias, verbosity bias, and self-enhancement bias. | Zheng et al. *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena.* NeurIPS 2023. [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2023/poster/73434) |
| S9 | RLHF assistants are consistently sycophantic: they shift toward user views and sometimes trade truth for agreement. | Sharma et al. *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models.* ICLR 2024. [ICLR](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/0105f7972202c1d4fb817da9f21a9663-Abstract-Conference.html) |
| S10 | Instructions planted in retrieved content (indirect prompt injection) compromise LLM-integrated applications. | Greshake et al. *Not what you've signed up for.* AISec 2023. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12173v2) |
| S11 | Assigning socio-demographic personas surfaces implicit stereotypes and increases reasoning errors; simple prompt de-biasing has minimal effect. | Gupta et al. *Bias Runs Deep.* ICLR 2024. [ICLR](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/17986) |
| S12 | LLM-written stories pass 3–10× fewer creativity tests than professionals' work; LLM evaluators did not correlate with expert assessments. | Chakrabarty et al. *Art or Artifice?* CHI 2024. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14556) |
| S13 | Professional writers agree on a 7-category taxonomy of AI-text idiosyncrasies (clichés, unnecessary exposition, purple prose, poor sentence structure, lack of specificity, awkward word choice, tense inconsistency). | Chakrabarty, Laban, Wu. *Can AI writing be salvaged?* CHI 2025. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14509v5) |
| S14 | AI-assisted stories are rated more creative individually but are more similar to each other (lower collective diversity). | Doshi, Hauser. Science Advances 10(28), 2024. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11244532) |
| S15 | Multi-agent failures cluster in specification issues, inter-agent misalignment, and task verification (MAST). | Cemri et al. *Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?* NeurIPS 2025. [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/121528) |

**Caveats**
- S2 is a workshop paper. Its practical recommendation ("include only what the agent can't discover") comes from secondary coverage, not text verified in the paper itself.
- S13's seven category names were confirmed from summaries of the paper; check the paper's taxonomy section before quoting them as exact labels.
- Considered and excluded:
  - Wang, Morgenstern, Dickerson on LLMs flattening identity groups: journal publication not confirmed in this pass.
  - Perry et al. on users writing less secure code with AI assistants: venue not confirmed in this pass.
